5️⃣ Architectural Decisions (ADR Summary)
ADR-001: Use Event-Driven Architecture

Decision: Use NATS as internal event bus.
Reason: Decouple services and handle asynchronous workflows.
Consequence: Requires event versioning discipline.

ADR-002: Merge Collector + Validator into Operations Service

Decision: Keep operational lifecycle in one service.
Reason: Avoid over-fragmentation and cross-service state sync.
Consequence: Service slightly larger but simpler to operate.

ADR-003: Keep IoT Service Separate

Decision: Isolate MQTT layer from core services.
Reason: IoT protocol concerns differ from business logic.
Consequence: Clear boundary between device world and domain world.

ADR-004: MySQL per Service

Decision: Each service owns its DB.
Reason: Avoid shared schema coupling.
Consequence: Cross-service joins prohibited.

ADR-005: Revenue Calculation in Business Service

Decision: Financial logic isolated from operations.
Reason: Prevent mixing operational state with accounting logic.
Consequence: Event-driven financial triggers.
